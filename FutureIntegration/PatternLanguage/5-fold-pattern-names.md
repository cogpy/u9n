# 5-fold Pattern Name Variants

Analogously equivalent pattern-name lists for the 253 patterns of the 5-fold pattern language. Each row is one pattern. `A001`–`A253` are the template names `p1001`–`p1253` in [`5-fold-patlan-x.md`](5-fold-patlan-x.md), unchanged. `P`, `S`, `C`, and `E` name that same relationship in the other four arenas.

The machine-readable crosswalk is [`5-fold-pattern-names.csv`](5-fold-pattern-names.csv).

## Subtypes

- **[A] Archetypal / Template.** Describes the pattern in content-free terms as pure relationship. A guideline for elaborating the pattern in other arenas. `A001`–`A253` = `p1001`–`p1253`.
- **[P] Physical / Architectural.** An adaptation of Christopher Alexander's own pattern names from *A Pattern Language* (1977), aligned one-to-one with the template (`P001` = pattern 1, `P253` = pattern 253).
- **[S] Social / Socio-Organizational.** The same relationship as it applies to the organization of social groups, organizations, and networks.
- **[C] Conceptual / Epistemological.** The same relationship as it applies to the organization of a conceptual framework or a body of knowledge.
- **[E] Experiential / Intra-Personal.** The same relationship as it applies to the organization of modes of awareness adopted by a person.

`S`, `C`, and `E` keep the relational skeleton of the template and replace its content-free nouns with the vocabulary of that arena (groups and ties; paradigms, descriptions, and frameworks; modes and fields of awareness). They are names, not reprints of the longer environment paragraphs. Where Alexander's title and the template already coincide (`Activity Nodes`, `Interchange`), the physical name is left as his.
